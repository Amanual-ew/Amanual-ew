## 💡 Featured Projects

### 🚗 Sheger Wheels: Peer-to-Peer Car Rental
A marketplace where Addis Ababa car owners list their vehicles and renters book by the day.

- Owner and renter dashboards, with availability and booking requests
- Admin approval of listings and a dispute system
- Reviews with owner replies
- Simulated TeleBirr checkout

**Stack:** Next.js · TypeScript · Prisma · PostgreSQL · NextAuth

[🌐 Live Demo](https://sheger-wheels-mu.vercel.app/) · [💻 Source Code](https://github.com/Amanual-ew/sheger-wheels)
